National Science Foundation
Smart and Connected Communities (S&CC)
Program Solicitation: AI-Enabled Water Sustainability and Climate Resilience for Underserved Communities

Synopsis
The National Science Foundation invites proposals for integrative, use-inspired research
that improves water sustainability and climate resilience in underserved communities.
Successful projects will pair advanced computing with environmental science and genuine
community engagement.

Areas of Interest
Proposals should combine several of the following capabilities:

- Artificial intelligence and machine learning for environmental data, including deep
  learning and natural language processing of policy and community input.
- Water resources, hydrology, drought, and water security, especially in arid regions.
- Sensor networks, the Internet of Things, and embedded systems for real-time
  environmental monitoring.
- Geospatial analysis, remote sensing, and climate data for decision support.
- Community-based participatory research, stakeholder engagement, and attention to
  health equity in underserved populations.
- Economic and policy analysis, including program evaluation and cost-benefit analysis
  of proposed interventions.
- Membrane desalination and water treatment technologies for reuse.
- Integration of indigenous knowledge systems in environmental decision-making.

Broader Impacts
Projects must broaden participation and include a credible plan for community
partnerships, workforce development, and translation of results into practice.

Awards
Anticipated total funding of $3,000,000 for approximately four awards. Interdisciplinary
teams spanning computing, engineering, environmental science, health, and the social
sciences are strongly encouraged.
