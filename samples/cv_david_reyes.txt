Research Interests
Applied microeconomics with an emphasis on program evaluation, policy analysis, and
cost-benefit analysis. I use econometrics and panel data methods, including
instrumental variables and randomized controlled trials, to evaluate the economic
impact of infrastructure and environmental policy.
