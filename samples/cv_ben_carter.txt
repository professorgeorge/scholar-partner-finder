Ben Carter

Research Interests
Water resources engineering, hydrology, and watershed management. I study drought
resilience and water security in arid regions, combining field data collection with
geospatial analysis and remote sensing. Recent work integrates low-cost sensor
networks for real-time streamflow monitoring.
